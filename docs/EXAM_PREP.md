# Exam and practical preparation

This is a general study workflow, not an official exam pattern or a prediction of questions.

## Before the exam
1. Confirm the current syllabus and assessment instructions with faculty.
2. Organize notes by module and list the algorithms taught in class.
3. Practise each lab program from a clean terminal.
4. Prepare a one-page summary for each algorithm: definition, steps, example, complexity, and limitation.
5. Revise evaluation metrics and common preprocessing mistakes.

## How to answer a 5-mark theory question
1. **Definition:** one precise opening statement.
2. **Working / steps:** ordered points, flow, or formula.
3. **Example:** a small illustrative case.
4. **Advantages and limitations:** only when relevant.
5. **Conclusion:** one sentence connecting the concept to its use.

## Practical viva checklist
- What is the problem being solved?
- What are the inputs, outputs, and assumptions?
- Why was this algorithm selected?
- What does each important variable represent?
- What happens for an empty or invalid input?
- How is the output checked?
- What is the time/space complexity?
- How could the program be improved?

## Last-day revision
Prioritize understanding the algorithms and examples actually covered in class. Use the repository's question bank as practice material, not as a guarantee of exam content.
