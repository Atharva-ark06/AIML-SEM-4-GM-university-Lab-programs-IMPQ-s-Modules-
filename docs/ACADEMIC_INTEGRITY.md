# Academic integrity and responsible use

This repository is a learning aid. Use it to understand concepts, practise implementation, and check your reasoning.

- Follow the university's rules on collaboration, citation, and permitted assistance.
- Do not submit another student's work as your own.
- Cite sources for borrowed explanations, figures, data, and code.
- Label synthetic data and illustrative results clearly.
- Never invent experimental results or claim that a program was tested when it was not.
- Check licensing and redistribution permission before uploading books, slides, or other third-party resources.
- Avoid sharing student IDs, personal contact details, credentials, or private classroom information.

When in doubt, ask the instructor what is permitted for the specific assignment.
